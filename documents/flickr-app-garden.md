## Description

Caterina is a free, native macOS app for your Flickr library, named for Caterina Fake, who co-founded Flickr.

- **Organize** — Flickr's Organizr rebuilt as batch edits: smart views (not in an album, untagged, no location, by licence, month, tag), a tray, and edits to titles, tags, who can see, safety, licence, dates, location, albums, groups and galleries. Each edit shows its cost in calls and time before it runs.
- **Undo** — every change can be undone. A delete waits 60 seconds before anything is sent, and anything undo cannot fully restore asks first.
- **Share to groups** — search your groups, see each group's limits, and share every photo, one group per photo, or best fit.
- **Download** — search Flickr, a photostream or a group, and save at the size you pick, with a Credits.csv naming photographer and licence.
- **Upload** — titles, captions and keywords from your files, presets, albums, and a queue that survives quitting.
- **Browse** — rankings, a timeline, a map, and daily stats kept past Flickr's 28 days.

You use your own free Flickr API key; it stays in your Mac's Keychain. Caterina collects nothing about you and talks only to Flickr.

Requires macOS 15.

This product uses the Flickr API but is not endorsed or certified by SmugMug, Inc.
